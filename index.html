Chili Rewards enabled

A grocer-facing offer intake layer that lets store managers or authorized associates review brand-funded opportunities, validate inventory, and move approved offers into GFT Rewards with Chili Rewards attached.

INPUTBrand offer
DECISIONStore + inventory fit
OUTPUTCampaign + CHI
01

How it works

Brand submits offerOffer enters the eligible-store inbox.
Store receives alertManager reviews product, mechanic, funding and timing.
Grocer checks inventoryInventory status gates acceptance.
AI structures campaignAccepted data is mapped into GFT Rewards fields.
Chili Rewards activatesApproved campaign includes CHI-based shopper rewards.
02

Core requirements

  • Prioritized offer inbox by store eligibility.
  • Search and category filtering.
  • Inventory status required before acceptance.
  • AI-generated campaign draft, not autonomous launch.
  • Chili Rewards configuration captured in the campaign.
  • Human approval before handoff into GFT Rewards.
03

Business outcome

  • Fewer manual campaign-setup steps.
  • Faster brand-to-store activation.
  • Offers matched to store inventory.
  • Closed-loop reward engagement through Chili Rewards.
